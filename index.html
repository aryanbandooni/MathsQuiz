HOW TO PLAY
  • There are 5 levels.
  • Each level has 30 questions.
  • You have 4 minutes for each level.
  • You must type your answers.
  • There are no MCQs.
  • Each correct answer gives 1 point.
  • Wrong answers give 0 points.
  • There is no negative marking.
  • You need at least 20/30 to clear a level.
  • If you fail, you can retry that level.
  • You can retry a level as many times as needed.
  • Previously cleared levels remain cleared.
  • Questions are shuffled every time you attempt a level.
  • Your reward is revealed only after you clear a level.
  • Complete all 5 levels to reach the Final Reward.

Speed matters.
Accuracy matters.
